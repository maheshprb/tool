# Media Studio
### *All-in-One Downloader & Media Converter Studio*

Crafted with care, elegance, and senior-friendly design so anyone can effortlessly download, convert, extract, and preview videos and audio from their phone or computer.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Universal Video Downloader**:
   - Downloads videos from **YouTube**, **Instagram** (Reels & posts), and **Facebook** (Watch & Reels).
   - In-app **live video preview** with player controls before saving.
   - Quality selection: Full HD 1080p, 720p HD, 480p, or best available quality.
   - Automatically detects the platform and highlights the corresponding badge.

2. **Link to Audio Extractor**:
   - Paste any YouTube, Instagram, or Facebook link and extract pure, studio-quality MP3 audio.
   - Live **Interactive Waveform Player**: scrub, play, pause, and preview the track directly in the browser before saving.
   - Bitrate options: 320 kbps (Studio), 192 kbps (Standard), 128 kbps (Compact).

3. **Video to Audio Converter**:
   - Upload any video file (.mp4, .mov, .mkv, .avi, .webm) from device camera roll or storage.
   - Converts to MP3, M4A, WAV, or FLAC.
   - In-app audio player preview before downloading.

4. **Audio to Video Converter**:
   - Turn any audio track or voice note into a shareable video for WhatsApp, Instagram, or YouTube.
   - Choose from **5 luxury aesthetic themes** (Midnight Obsidian, Warm Sunset, Emerald Velvet, Deep Sapphire, Silk Noir) or upload a **custom photo**.
   - Select dimensions: **16:9 Landscape** (YouTube/TV), **9:16 Vertical** (Reels/Shorts), or **1:1 Square** (Instagram Posts).
   - Watch the rendered video right inside the app before saving!

5. **Classy & Senior-Friendly UI/UX**:
   - Warm Obsidian & Champagne Gold aesthetic — zero cluttered AI-slop or boxy cards.
   - Large, clear font sizes and generous tap targets (>48px) for effortless mobile touch.
   - One-tap **"Paste"** button directly in the input box so you don't have to fiddle with mobile menus.
   - PWA ready: tap "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone (Safari) or Android (Chrome) to install it like a native app.

## 🚀 How to Launch

### Option 1: Double-Click Launcher (Windows)
Simply double-click:
```
start.bat
```

### Option 2: Command Line
```powershell
python run.py
```

The app will start and automatically open in your default browser at:
`http://localhost:8000`

## 📱 How Your Father Can Use It on His Phone

1. Make sure your father's phone is connected to the same home Wi-Fi network as the computer.
2. In the top right corner of the app, click **"Use on Phone"**.
3. A QR code will appear on the screen.
4. Have your father open the Camera app on his iPhone or Android and point it at the QR code.
5. Tap the link that pops up (or type `http://192.168.29.130:8000` in Safari/Chrome).
6. **Bonus Tip**: In Safari (iOS) tap **Share ➔ Add to Home Screen**, or in Chrome (Android) tap the menu **➔ Add to Home screen / Install app**. It will appear right on his phone with the gold Aura Media icon just like a native app!
